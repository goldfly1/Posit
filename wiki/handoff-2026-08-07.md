## Lessons Learned

1. **Model can write Dafny** — deepseek-v4-pro:cloud verified 2/5 modules from scratch. With skeletons on disk, success rate should improve.
2. **JSON extraction is the fragile point** — model wraps Dafny in prose, reasoning tags, or `files[]` format. Multiple fallback parsers needed.
3. **Partial verification is essential** — all-or-nothing risks losing proven modules on non-deterministic re-runs.
4. **Wiki search works** — 2,147 Dafny examples give the model proven patterns to imitate.
5. **`dafny translate cs` produces verbose C#** — uses Dafny runtime wrappers. Post-processing may be needed.
6. **Dafny strings aren't nullable** — `requires value != null` fails for `seq<char>`. The model needs syntax guidance (already added to prompt).
7. **`type` is a reserved keyword in Dafny** — can't use as a parameter name. Model hit this error.
8. **Standard libraries are available** — `import Std.Collections.Seq`, `Std.Wrappers`, etc. with `--standard-libraries` flag.
9. **Dafny compiles to 6 targets** — C#, Rust, Go, Java, JS, Python. One proof, portable deployment.
10. **I/O boundary is the proof boundary** — pure logic in Dafny, side effects in C#. The function signature is the membrane.
